Changes draft scores

Synergy scores use observed minus anchor-baseline expected win rate. Matchup scores use observed win rate against that enemy; they are not corrected for each champion's baseline. Both are shrunk toward zero using game counts and capped, to avoid overpowering player comfort. These are heuristics, not true win-chance predictions.

Source transparency & limits

Read before relying on draft scores
  • U.GG: patch 26.20, World, Emerald+, Ranked Solo; 15 imported role-specific opponent tables. An entry is our champion's observed win rate against the named enemy, derived as 100% minus the opposing champion's listed win rate.
  • RiftMind: duo/allied-role performance from patches 26.19–26.20, Ranked Solo/Duo; low and high elo kept separate. Delta means observed win rate minus anchor-baseline expected win rate. Not an absolute win-rate boost.
  • Historical 26.18 records are in the Interaction filter for Lee Sin, Jhin, and Rakan, independently of the 26.20 counter snapshot and the 26.19–20 synergy window. This covers observations touching the three recent patches but is not a combined three-patch statistical estimate; the historical rows are browse-only. On U.GG, you may need to select patch 26.18 manually because source URLs default to the latest patch.
  • Data is a fixed offline snapshot checked 2026-10-19. The app does not fetch new patches, track champion changes, or replace your team-specific judgment. Some champions have no imported matchup table; they receive no statistical effect, not a fabricated average.
  • Sources: U.GG data FAQ · RiftMind methodology. Starter manual team rules stay disabled until you enable them.

What the app actually does

Transparent heuristics; not a trained model.

1 · Pool legality

Only recommends a champion listed for that player in the assigned lane. It excludes all enemy picks, bans, and already-locked allied picks. Auto compares default and swapped TOP/MID configurations, rejecting ones that conflict with locked picks.

2 · Blind-pick safety

B champs get a safety bonus with hidden enemy lanes. B! champs are held out while blind unless approved in the pool editor or you turn off strict filtering. Unmarked picks are allowed with a blind-risk penalty; they are not automatically “unblindable.”

3 · Player comfort

Ratings 1–5 are entirely yours. All entries start with neutral 3/5 and show unrated. Real ratings are the single biggest improvement you can add. The app does not pretend to know who can actually pilot each champion.

4 · Team composition

Starter labels cover frontline, engage, peel, poke, scaling and damage type. They are subjective placeholders you can validate. Explicit priorities add score to matching champions. Existing picks influence missing roles like frontline or physical damage.

5 · Seth / David decision

Default Seth TOP + David MID gets a small preference. Seth MID on Zed, Yasuo or Yone earns a situational bonus against a weaker mid. David TOP on Ornn or Malphite earns a bonus against a stronger top. These are configurable rules, not matchup predictions.

6 · Champion interactions

Enabled pair synergies add 4 points for each strength level. Matchup rules add or subtract 5 points per level. Counters can apply against the enemy in the same role or against any enemy slot. Only revealed enemy picks affect scores. Patch-specific measured statistics are imported and labeled in the Patch statistics tab. Heuristic score changes are not win probabilities; old (1)–(4), * and F remain unused.
